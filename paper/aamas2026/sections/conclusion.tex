\section{Conclusion}

\system evaluates and improves the runtime reliability of long-horizon multi-agent workflows. Rather than introducing a new dataset, it layers production stress, contractual monitoring, fault localization, bounded recovery, and event-sourced tracing on top of public benchmarks. The current artifact establishes the runtime kernel, stress harness, baselines, stressors, trace-derived metrics, ablations, figures, and paper tables. The next step is to execute the external benchmark matrix and replace smoke artifacts with full tau2-bench / tau3-bench and AgentChangeBench results.
